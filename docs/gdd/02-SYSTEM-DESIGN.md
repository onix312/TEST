# Do Not Feed the Cargo — System Design

**Baseline:** approved decisions GDD-001—100; see [register](DECISIONS-001-100.md). **Document purpose:** interfaces, invariants, integration risks. Implementation architecture below is **proposed**, not separately approved.

## Simulation boundaries

**Game simulation (Unity C#):** authoritative gameplay state for actors/vehicles/creatures, contracts, equipment, company data; deterministic IDs for objects; physical interactions, events and save snapshots. Prefer a single logical authoritative host for alpha in alignment with GDD-011; exact transport and physics-prediction technology must be benchmarked before selection.

**Persistent company state:** garage layout, fleet ownership/rental/loan/damage, staff skills and relationships, storage physical inventory, finance, permits, blueprints, story/faction changes. **Per-expedition state:** generated road graph, weather/time, spawned incidents, physical cargo and vehicle state, current contracts, party status. **Cross-trip abstraction:** NPC and creature history, reputation and unresolved consequences, without a continuously running persistent open world.

**Proposed state interface:** `CompanySave`, `ExpeditionSave`, `WorldHistory` with schema versions, stable IDs, atomic save files and recoverable backups. Create migration tests before changing persisted formats.

## Physical player controller (GDD-049—058)

- First-person controller: walk/run/crouch/jump/slide with responsive inputs; slope, friction and carried mass influence motion.
- Two independent hands with server-validated grab/release, carry, push/pull, shared mass transfer and physical throwing; accessible contextual assistance positions a grab, not replaces simulation with a canned action.
- Climbing is *standard contextual climbing* at suitable obstacles/designated surfaces (055), not unlimited hand-over-hand everywhere.
- Short-term exertion influences carrying, sprinting and grip; soft long-term fatigue is separate.
- Ragdoll activates on sufficiently violent physical events; partial hand/body control and physical teammate rescue; full first-person head-follow camera continues through tumbles **without mandatory horizon stabilizer** (057).
- Passenger physics: inertia of standing occupants and unsecured cargo, restraints and grabbable rails; secure seat occupancy and role hand-off. Open-door vehicle-to-vehicle interactions remain physically possible even in motion.

**Acceptance tests:** collision/carry invariants for 1/2/4 players; no cargo teleportation; reliable joint release on disconnect; controlled ragdoll recovery; no camera detach; safe restoration after reconnect.

## Vehicle system (025—031, 058—063)

Enclosed cargo vans only; operational cabin and hold, cab-hold passage, rear and side access, modular stations/hardpoints, physical loose cargo and secure straps. Car dynamics are semi-arcade **controlled by input** while mass, balance, traction and subsystems remain physically significant. Transmission is **automatic only** (060).

Model subsystems independently: wheel/tires; powertrain/fuel; suspension; brakes; coolant; electrics; chassis/panels; doors; cargo fixtures. Vehicle-dependent operating envelopes, fuel use and repair material/time. Damage generated by actual collisions and failure conditions; recovery via repairs, NPC tow or costly evacuation. Multiple vans may operate together for major trips; per-vehicle entity ownership, streaming and snapshot rules required.

**Proposed interface:** `VehicleState`, `VehicleModule`, `CargoAnchor`, `PassengerRestraint`, `VehicleDamageEvent`. Avoid relying solely on Unity rigidbody default interpolation for network-authoritative cargo in moving vehicles; prototype parenting/contact/velocity transfer and correct snapshots first.

## Creature simulation (017—024, 083)

Every species has species-level physical abilities plus individual needs/stress/fear/trust/memory and goals. Creature **AI knows only perceived world state**, sensory triggers and environmental feedback. Threat and comfort signals readable through observation and optional sensors; no privileged UI mental-state feed. Cage, humidity/temperature/ventilation and physical obstacles constrain motion. Species interactions may help or harm; unexpected escape is not scripted omnipotence.

**Behavior layers, proposed:** perception → needs evaluation → short-horizon goal selector → physically feasible action → feedback/memory. Species feature components are data-driven and testable. Critical injuries/death have permanent consequences, including legal/contract impact.

## Procedural expedition (001—006, 035—043)

Seeded road graphs with intersections and accessible main route; hidden branches and optional POIs. Author-created modular POI templates vary in positions, contents, state. Multiple biomes can occur in one route. Basic off-road traversal is possible when physically traversable; neither generator nor event director may place mandatory goal behind an untraversable obstacle without recoverable alternate means.

Event director may pace *candidate* events but cannot bypass NPC perception, travel, communication or physically created consequences. Intelligence can be stale/false; players annotate personal/shared physical maps by physical action.

**Generator quality gates, proposed:** graph connectivity, reachable mission objective for foot/vehicle where required, recoverable route alternatives, stable seeds for QA, consistent junction markings and GPS/cartography fog-of-knowledge.

## NPC world (035—048, 064—080)

Separate ordinary traffic AI from persistent significant NPCs. Significant agents: goal, vehicle/inventory, schedule limited to active sim, faction affiliation, known evidence, memory and social incentives. Contacts transmit evidence via actual shouts, radio, witnesses and networked communication. Police do not spawn based on a universal wanted level. Legal proceedings/arrest are playable and recoverable; property stored physically in confiscation sites.

Support workers only: mechanic, veterinarian, dispatcher, warehouse staff. They observe issues, prioritize authorized jobs, respect resource caps, acquire materials physically, rest and can be injured or resign. **Do not schedule autonomous NPC delivery crews for core contracts** (075).

## Company, inventory and crafting (072—091)

- Accounting: contracts/payments, per-company debt, lending/pledges, insurance coverage and claims, rent/lease/ownership, consequences but recovery routes.
- Garage: modular expansion; freely positioned physical stations/cages/shelves; power/water/air/climate/security service networks via modular connections, resource costs, failures and repairs.
- Physical warehouse: stable object IDs, registered positions, scan events and item reservations; records **may be stale** and cannot move objects.
- Suppliers: actual stock and traveling NPC deliveries, claims for defective goods, delegated restocking with hard budgets.
- Manufacturing: material inputs, physical workstations, blueprints, modular construction, quality/hidden defects; repeatable work delegated to qualified support NPC.
- R&D: reverse engineering, instrumented prototype tests and improvement loops; do not calculate test success as perfect safety.
- Salvage: recover components with condition/latent risk and properly handle dangerous waste.

**Critical integration invariant:** purchasing, crafting, scanning or insurance payout may change economic records, never teleport a physical creature/cage/tool or magically repair a vehicle. Every physical item transfer requires a corresponding event in simulation.

## Networking, performance and QA architecture (proposal)

1. Prove authoritative objects and ownership transfers before content scaling.
2. Snapshot/rollback or equivalent robustness for moving-vehicle payloads, player grabbing and Ragdoll, with jitter/packet-loss tests.
3. Area-based physics activation and NPC simulation LOD preserve **causal** consequences; don't simulate full remote rigidbodies indefinitely.
4. Use recorded deterministic scenarios where possible, with explicit seeds and event traces; statistical variability for networked physics may require tolerance-based assertions.
5. Persist pending operations atomically; backup before schema migrations; test corruption/recovery, reconnect and host-save-exit.
6. Build observability: structured events for cargo transfers, contract transitions, NPC info sharing, vehicle damage, staff task authorization, decisions and save checkpoints.

## Non-negotiable negative requirements

No open-bed pickup replacing the closed van; no constant full-screen magic map; no completely manual gearbox; no unlimited free-surface climbing; no stabilized Ragdoll camera as default requirement; no NPC delivery crews replacing player contracts; no offline simulation; no mainline vertical-slice reduction of GDD-099.
